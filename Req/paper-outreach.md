GTM Agent – Feature Ideas
1. Research Paper Outreach

Goal: Automate outbound outreach to research authors while keeping the user in control.

Workflow

Accept one or more research papers (PDF, DOI, or arXiv link).
Extract:
Title
Abstract
Authors
Research topics
Find author profiles across platforms:
LinkedIn
X (Twitter)
Personal website
Google Scholar
University profile
Generate personalized outreach:
Email draft
LinkedIn connection note
X DM (if applicable)
Wait for user approval before sending.
Send outreach through connected accounts.
Track replies and automatically:
Follow up after configurable delays.
Stop follow-ups once a response is received.
Schedule meetings when interest is shown.
Sync meetings with calendar.
